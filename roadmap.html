Roadmap

From idea to licensed CT operator.

Sixth Sense is not rushing into the market. There is a deliberate sequence: learn the landscape, lock in the structure, then apply and execute when the timing and regulations line up.

Milestones

Phase 1 · Now
Research & planning
Analyze CT regulations, licensing patterns, zoning realities and market gaps. Translate that into a realistic operating and financial model for a micro-cultivator.
Phase 2
Entity, structure & capital
Form Sixth Sense Cannabis LLC, finalize ownership and governance, and partner with aligned capital that understands patient, infrastructure-first growth.
Phase 3
License window
The moment the next CT micro-cultivator lottery opens, submit a complete, thoughtful application package: business plan, security plan, zoning support, financials and operating model.
Phase 4
Facility build-out
Secure an industrial space in a compliant CT municipality and complete a build-out that balances efficiency, environmental control and security from day one.
Phase 5
Launch & refine
Once fully licensed and operational, ship the first small-batch runs, gather real feedback from CT consumers and retail partners, and steadily refine toward best-in-market quality.

What “ready” means to us

For Sixth Sense, “ready” is not a logo and an Instagram handle. It means:

  • ✓ A fully drafted application binder before the window opens.
  • ✓ Shortlisted industrial locations with real power and zoning viability.
  • ✓ Clear SOPs that satisfy regulators and make sense in day-to-day operations.
  • ✓ Capital committed for build-out, staffing and initial operating runway.

Until those boxes are checked, Sixth Sense stays focused on preparation, not pressure.
