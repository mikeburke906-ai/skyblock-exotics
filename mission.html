Mission

We’re here to build the brand we wish already existed.

Sixth Sense was created out of frustration with mid-quality, investor-driven cannabis dominating Connecticut shelves. The mission is direct: when we are licensed, our product will feel like it was designed for people who live here and actually care about what goes in their lungs.

Quality over volume Serious about compliance Respect for the consumer

Mission statement

Sixth Sense Cannabis exists to become a leading CT-based micro-cultivator that pairs disciplined, regulation-first operations with product that clearly reflects care, intention and pride. The goal isn’t to be the biggest name in the room — it’s to be the one people trust when quality actually matters.

How we will operate once licensed

  • ✓ Small-batch, tightly controlled production instead of anonymous mass volume.
  • ✓ Transparent, accurate testing data and clear communication with retail partners.
  • ✓ Systems and SOPs that prioritize consumer safety, consistency and repeatability.
  • ✓ Pricing that respects both the cost of doing things the right way and the customer’s wallet.

Principles that don’t move

Sixth Sense is being designed to stay grounded, even as the market shifts.

  • 1 No shortcuts with the law. Every step is aligned with Connecticut regulations.
  • 2 Real smokers in the decision room. Product decisions should be made by people who actually use it.
  • 3 Long-term thinking. Infrastructure, not quick flips.
